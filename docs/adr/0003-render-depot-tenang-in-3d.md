# Render Depot Tenang in 3D

Depot Tenang now loads Blender GLB models and renders its Diorama with Three.js. Rapier 3D simulates loose stones and blocks; trucks and trains follow guided paths so a young child can still complete each Activity with taps. This replaces the layered 2D and Phaser decisions in ADR 0001 and ADR 0002 because the vehicle models and their motion must hold up on large screens while the Game remains playable on phones.
