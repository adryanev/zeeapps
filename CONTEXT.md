# Dunia Zee

This context describes a laptop play experience for a young child. The child can explore independently, while family members can naturally join the same activity.

## Language

**Dunia Zee**:
The public Playroom where families access the same growing collection of Games.
_Avoid_: Zeeapps, toddler app

**Playroom**:
The form of the hosted website that contains Games and separates Companion choices from the Child Stage.
_Avoid_: Platform, portal, family space

**Open access**:
Access to the Playroom without an account, child profile, or household-specific content.
_Avoid_: Guest mode, anonymous account

**Game**:
A self-contained play activity inside the Playroom. Each family accesses the same Game and its shared content.
_Avoid_: Lesson, module, personalized experience

**Explorer**:
The child who controls the play experience and chooses what to investigate.
_Avoid_: Student, learner, end user

**Companion**:
A family member who joins the Explorer during play without taking control of the activity.
_Avoid_: Teacher, supervisor, operator

**Independent exploration**:
Play in which the Explorer discovers and repeats interactions without step-by-step help from a Companion. It does not mean the Explorer must be alone.
_Avoid_: Solo mode, unsupervised play

**Co-play**:
A shared Play Session in which an Explorer and Companion react, imitate, point, or talk together.
_Avoid_: Assisted mode, parent mode

**Co-presence**:
A Play Session in which a Companion stays nearby and available while the Explorer controls the Game independently.
_Avoid_: Unsupervised play, babysitting mode

**Play Session**:
A period of open exploration that may move naturally between Independent exploration and Co-play.
_Avoid_: Lesson, level, exercise

**Play Cycle**:
A repeatable story arc inside a Play Session that reaches a calm stopping point without declaring success or failure.
_Avoid_: Round, level, challenge

**Child Stage**:
The menu-free part of a Game where the Explorer controls play through safe keyboard and pointer actions.
_Avoid_: Game page, child mode

**Companion Gate**:
An intentional action that lets a Companion enter or leave the Child Stage without presenting ordinary navigation to the Explorer.
_Avoid_: Exit button, parental controls

**Calm play**:
Predictable, low-pressure play without scores, countdowns, or failure states.
_Avoid_: Challenge mode, test

**Depot Tenang**:
The first Game in Dunia Zee, a calm vehicle world where trucks, trains, and airplanes arrive, move, and settle down.
_Avoid_: Vehicle lesson, driving game

**Diorama**:
Depot Tenang's shared side-view world, with a road in front, railway in the middle, and sky above.
_Avoid_: Map, level, separate scene

**Active Vehicle**:
The single vehicle that currently responds to the Explorer's keyboard and pointer actions in the Depot.
_Avoid_: Selected object, player character

**Resting Place**:
A vehicle's recognizable home in the Diorama, such as the garage, station, or hangar.
_Avoid_: Goal, finish line, target

**Vehicle Journey**:
The three-part path of an Active Vehicle as it arrives, performs its characteristic activity, and returns to its Resting Place.
_Avoid_: Task, mission, stage

**Quiet State**:
The end of a Play Cycle, when all vehicles rest and further input produces only small, gentle responses.
_Avoid_: Game over, completion screen, idle mode

**Guided Physics**:
Physical movement that preserves weight, momentum, and soft collisions while keeping every object inside a safe, playable range.
_Avoid_: Full simulation, scripted animation

**Soft Grab**:
A forgiving pointer interaction that pulls a physical object with gentle lag instead of attaching it rigidly to the pointer.
_Avoid_: Precise drag-and-drop, direct positioning

**Equivalent Input**:
Different physical actions that express the same play intention across devices, such as a keyboard press or an empty-area tap advancing a Vehicle Journey.
_Avoid_: Mobile mode, desktop mode

**Expressive Gesture**:
An optional swipe or movement that adds a physical response without being required to continue a Vehicle Journey.
_Avoid_: Required gesture, gesture challenge

**Gentle Recovery**:
The quiet return of an object that has become stuck, inverted, or unreachable to a playable position.
_Avoid_: Reset, error correction, respawn

**Companion Prompt**:
A short suggestion shown before a Game that gives the Companion one way to talk, imitate, or point together with the Explorer.
_Avoid_: Instruction, tutorial, learning objective

**Familiar Voice**:
A family-provided recording that names an Active Vehicle with one short Indonesian word and may be heard by every family using Dunia Zee.
_Avoid_: Narrator, synthetic voice, baby talk

**Sound Profile**:
The Companion's device-local choice between Lembut, Normal, and Senyap for all Game sounds.
_Avoid_: Audio mode, child volume control

**Reduced Motion**:
A device-local Companion preference that reduces incidental sway, parallax, and physical impulses without changing a Vehicle Journey.
_Avoid_: Easy mode, static mode

**2.5D art**:
Layered two-dimensional illustration that uses perspective, depth, and soft shadows while remaining made from flat image assets.
_Avoid_: 3D model, flat icon
